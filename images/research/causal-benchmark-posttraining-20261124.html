Causal Benchmark and Post-training

TL;DR: Can language models carry out causal analysis reliably, and can post-training improve open models without damaging other skills? We combine Causal Bench, which checks answers and analysis code on causal tasks with known ground truth, with two post-training runs on a 27B open model. The benchmark exposes gaps between frontier and open models. Training raises accuracy on our in-domain test, but also harms tool use and performance on an external causal test. We trace a tool-use failure to training examples that always make exactly one call. Targeted examples for zero-call and multi-call cases recover much of the lost tool performance while preserving the in-domain gain. The lesson is to judge training by both what improves and what it preserves: broader benchmark gains remain unestablished, and RL adds no measurable in-domain improvement in these runs.
