Intelligent Systems

Leon studies intelligent systems at the intersection of AI and economics. He uses controlled experiments to study how AI agents collaborate and allocate computation, trains and evaluates models for causal reasoning, and designs incentive mechanisms for decentralized insurance.

About Me

Leon Leng is a Ph.D. student in Information Systems & Operations Management at the University of Washington’s Foster School of Business, with minors in Computer Science and Economics. He is also a visiting researcher at Microsoft Research Asia and previously worked as an Elite (Qingyun) Research Scientist with Tencent’s WeChat Experiment Platform. He holds a B.S. in Data Science from The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen, and studied EECS as a visiting student at UC Berkeley. Feel free to reach out for a conversation or collaboration.
